SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SAMPLE — SYNTHETIC)
Between Northwind Analytics LLC ("Provider") and Contoso Dental Group PC ("Customer"). This SLA supplements the Master Services Agreement.

1. Availability. The document processing appliance will be available 99.5% of each calendar month, measured at the appliance's health endpoint, excluding scheduled maintenance.
2. Scheduled Maintenance. Provider may schedule maintenance windows of up to four (4) hours per month, with at least seventy-two (72) hours notice, between 10 PM and 6 AM Pacific.
3. Support Response. Severity 1 (service down): response within two (2) hours, 24x7. Severity 2 (degraded): response within eight (8) business hours. Severity 3 (question): response within two (2) business days.
4. Service Credits. If monthly availability falls below 99.5%, Customer receives a credit of 10% of that month's service fee; below 99.0%, a credit of 25%. Credits are the sole remedy for availability failures.
5. Backups. Customer is responsible for scheduling and verifying backups using the provided backup tooling. Provider will assist with restore procedures within one (1) business day of a request.
6. Model Updates. Provider will publish signed model and software updates no less than quarterly. Customer controls when updates are applied.
7. Reporting. Provider will deliver a monthly availability and support report by the fifth (5th) business day of the following month.
